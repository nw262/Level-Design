Prefabs come ready to use in unity, otherwise most meshes 
require a material to be re-assigned and saved as a new Prefab.

Alternatively, if a texture map is required, 
assign the texture to mesh/model followed by any other required
texture maps (eg. emissive, transmission, normal etc.) map and/or texture map to be 
re-assigned and saved as new Prefab.
